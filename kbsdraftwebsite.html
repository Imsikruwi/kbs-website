Empowering Your Strategic Vision in Indonesia

PT Konsultasi Bisnis Strategis (PT. KBS) is a premier, Indonesian-owned consultancy firm dedicated to navigating complex strategic issues at the highest echelons of business. We serve as a trusted advisor to both governmental and non-governmental entities, bridging gaps and unlocking potential within the dynamic Indonesian market.

KBS is active in the fields of strategic business consultancy, strategic economic consultancy (including banking e.g. Credit Investment) and strategic political consultancy.

Our focus is to support foreign Companies, mainly multi-national Companies, that are leading in their business sector in the world market.

Who We Are

About Us

Operational since January 1, 2025, PT. KBS was founded on the principles of excellence, integrity, and profound market insight. Our firm is led by a distinguished board of Shareholders and Directors, all of whom possess advanced degrees from world-renowned universities in the United States and the United Kingdom. This exceptional blend of global perspective and deep local expertise empowers us to deliver world-class advisory services tailored to the unique landscape of Indonesia.
